## The 1-7 canine fecal scale
This is a paraphrased summary. The chart rates canine stool from 1 (hardest) to 7 (most liquid) by how it looks and how it behaves when picked up.
Score 1: very hard and dry, often individual pellets, no residue left behind.
Score 2: firm but not hard, pliable, segmented appearance, little or no residue.
Score 3: log-like with little or no segmentation, moist surface, leaves some residue but holds its form.
Score 4: very moist (soggy), distinct log shape, leaves residue and loses form when picked up.
Score 5: very moist but with a distinct shape, present in piles rather than distinct logs.
Score 6: has texture but no defined shape, occurs as piles or spots.
Score 7: watery, no texture, flat, occurs as puddles.

## Interpreting scores
The document lists the seven descriptions and does not itself state an ideal score. PawPal+ AI therefore treats scores 2-3 as the typical well-formed range and does not claim an official ideal. A single photograph cannot capture behaviour such as residue left on the ground, so any photo-based score is an estimate.
