## Possible contributors (paraphrased)
Short-lived diarrhea is often associated with dietary indiscretion, switching foods too quickly, stress such as boarding, and internal parasites. More serious possibilities include infection or inflammation, viruses such as parvovirus, foreign bodies, allergies, toxins and organ disease. A photograph cannot tell these apart.

## Red flags
The article advises seeing a veterinarian when diarrhea lasts more than a day or two or does not resolve within roughly 48-72 hours, when stool is black, tarry or contains fresh blood, when vomiting accompanies diarrhea (dehydration risk), or when appetite loss or lethargy persists.

## Diet transitions
If loose stool began after a food switch, the article advises returning to the previous diet until stools firm up and then repeating the change more slowly, spread over a week or two.
