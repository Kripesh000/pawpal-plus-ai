## Cats use a different scale
This is a paraphrased summary. The feline chart uses a 1 to 5 scale (with a half-point at 2.5), from 1 (formed and dry) to 5 (liquid). It is not the same scale as the canine 1-7 system, and the numbers are not interchangeable.
Score 1: hard, dry, crumbly. Score 2: formed, hard, clearly cracked. Score 2.5: formed, firm, slightly damp surface. Score 3: unformed, soft but keeps some shape, leaves residue on litter. Score 4: very soft, very wet but not liquid. Score 5: liquid or nearly liquid with no texture.

## What PawPal+ AI does for cats
PawPal+ AI does NOT assign a numeric score to cats. It reports only a descriptive consistency category (for example firm, soft, loose, watery). A feline numeric scale was deliberately not implemented because the prototype's image features were not validated against it.
