## What visible mucus can be associated with (paraphrased)
Mucus typically looks like shiny slime, clear jelly or a coating around the stool, and is often associated with colon irritation or inflammation. Associations listed include intestinal infections, parasites, dietary indiscretion, abrupt diet change or food intolerance, stress-related bowel upset and inflammatory bowel disease. Many of these cannot be told apart by looking.

## When it is more concerning
A small amount is described as not a reason to panic. It is more concerning when there is a lot of mucus, when it keeps appearing, or when it comes with diarrhea, blood, poor appetite, weight loss, vomiting or pain. Very young, very old or already-ill dogs warrant a prompt call to the veterinarian.
