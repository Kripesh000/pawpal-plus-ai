## Colour overview (paraphrased)
Chocolate brown is described as typical when consistency is also typical. Yellow or orange colouring is described as possibly related to the liver or biliary system. White or grey stool is described as possibly related to incomplete digestion of nutrients. Rice-like white specks may be associated with tapeworm segments. Red or blood-stained stool suggests bleeding, possibly from straining. Black or tarry stool may indicate bleeding higher in the digestive tract (stomach or small intestine).
The article recommends speaking to a veterinarian as soon as possible about red, black, yellow/orange, or white/grey stool, and about persistent diarrhoea or consistently hard stool.

## Caveats
This is a consumer-education source, not a clinical reference. Colour can also be affected by diet, treats and supplements. A photograph cannot confirm the cause of any colour change, and lighting changes how colours appear.
