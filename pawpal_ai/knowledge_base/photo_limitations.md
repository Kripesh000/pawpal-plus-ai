## Limits of photo-based monitoring
This note is the project's own summary of the safety framing required by the assignment, not a published reference. Parasites and their eggs, bacteria, viruses and microscopic (occult) blood generally cannot be identified from a photograph. Lighting, camera white balance, background colour, moisture and diet colourants all change how stool appears. Photo observations are for tracking trends over time and for deciding whether to contact a veterinarian. Only a veterinary professional can diagnose a condition.
